### Guardrail changes: what each one does

- **The auto-merge stop, and the policy's auto-merge switch, hold direct landing too (#808).** Direct landing never checked the auto-merge stop. So a stop left from PR mode (main went red, say) was ignored after a switch to `land_mode: direct`, and approved changes landed on the branch the stop was protecting. A direct land now checks the stop, the same check PR mode makes, before its gates and again right before its push. While it is set, the queue waits until the operator clears it. And with the instance's `policy.yaml` `auto_merge: off`, or a policy that can't be read, only what the owner approved (L3) lands; automatic landing (L0 to L2) waits queued. A local site, which has no instance policy, lands as before.

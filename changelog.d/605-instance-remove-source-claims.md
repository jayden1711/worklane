### Upgrading (from v0.5.2)

- **`instance-remove.sh` and `instance-rename.sh` now take the instance's Slack and Linear claims with its Discord one (#605).** Each source writes its claim to `/var/lib/worklane/agent-slots/<kind>-claim-<name>.json`, and until now only Discord's was in the removal registry, so a removed or renamed instance left its Slack and Linear claims behind. A removal now deletes all three by exact path, plus any copy of one that a crash left mid-write (`<kind>-claim-<name>.json.<pid>.<hex>.tmp`). A rename drops the old name's claims, and the new name's coordinator writes its own when its source starts. Claims left by an instance removed before this release stay where they are. Root may delete them by hand; they hold only a hash, a pid and the instance's name.
